## Step 3 prompt: clean the scrape

```
Role: P1. Follow CLAUDE.md and CONTEXT.md first. Check status/DECISIONS.md for the data-terms decision and exclude any source marked EXCLUDE.

Write and run src/clean.py. Input: raw scraper outputs in data/raw/ (inspect and print their columns first; data/raw is gitignored, never commit it). Output: data/clean/items.csv exactly per CONTRACT.md, plus data/clean/coverage_report.csv.

Rules:
1. Reddit: date from created_utc (exact). date_low = date_high = that date. banner_hint from subreddit name or post flair if it names a banner (Dick's, House of Sport, Golf Galaxy, Foot Locker).
2. Google: parse relative dates against scraped_at into an interval, never a point:
   "N days ago" -> [scraped - (N+1) days, scraped - (N-1) days], precision week
   "a week ago" / "N weeks ago" -> [scraped - (N+1)*7 days, scraped - N*7 days], precision week
   "a month ago" / "N months ago" -> [scraped - (N+1) months, scraped - N months], precision month
   "a year ago" / "N years ago" -> [scraped - (N+1) years, scraped - N years], precision year
   Strip "Edited" prefixes. Keep star ratings in stars. banner_hint from the store/location name.
3. Drop exact duplicates, near-duplicates (identical after lowercasing and removing punctuation and whitespace), and empty texts. Keep short reviews.
4. Drop all usernames, profile links, and author fields.
5. item_id = source + "_" + a stable hash of url + text.
6. coverage_report.csv: counts by calendar quarter x source x date_precision. Print the earliest and latest date_low per source and the share of Google items with precision "year".
Print rows in, rows out, and rows dropped by each rule. Commit items.csv (if under 50 MB; otherwise tell P2 in status), then add "HANDOFF: items.csv ready (N rows, date range X to Y)" to status/P1.md and push.
```

## Step 4 prompt: GenAI appendix

```
Role: P1. Follow CLAUDE.md and CONTEXT.md first. Read status/*.md, outputs/metrics.csv and outputs/nowcast.json if present, data/labels/agreement.json if present, and docs/compliance.md.

Draft docs/appendix/appendix.md: content for 3 appendix slides for the Point72 case. The rules require: all publicly offered GenAI tools used; primary use cases with prompt/response examples; and one worked example showing the prompt, the GenAI response, the gap or limitation identified, the additional public research performed, and how the conclusion changed. Structure each slide as Objective / Method and data source / Output snapshot / Interpretation / Limitations and bias mitigation. Max 80 words of body text per slide.

Facts to use:
- Tools: [list: Claude, ChatGPT, Laya (open source, self-hosted), others used]
- Pipeline: scraped text -> 7 frozen Laya questions -> quarterly indices -> expanding-window backtest vs AR(1) and a star-rating baseline -> one flagged comp driver in our own Excel model.
- Worked example: first-round research proposed daily stock-price models (StockNet, MAN-SF). We inspected their code and published results and found such models lose to a "price stays the same" baseline and predict prices, not fundamentals. Follow-up research pointed to text-based nowcasting of quarterly fundamentals (Okuneva et al.) and Huang (2018) on consumer reviews predicting revenue surprises. Our question changed from "predict the stock" to "nowcast DICK'S comps before the Q3 print".
- Validation: Laya vs human agreement on 50 hand-labeled items (fill from agreement.json; leave a placeholder if missing).
- Limitations: reviews and Reddit skew to extreme experiences, enthusiasts, and employees; Google dates are coarse intervals; short history (K quarters); questions and spec frozen before results.
- Data collection and compliance: summarize docs/compliance.md.
Flag any sentence that overclaims. Commit, then add "HANDOFF: appendix text ready" to status/P1.md.
```
