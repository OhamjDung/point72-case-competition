## 2. Business, recent events, catalysts

**Asset.** Deucrictibant is an oral, small-molecule bradykinin B2 receptor antagonist (a pill version of the icatibant mechanism) for HAE and related bradykinin-mediated angioedema. Two formulations: an immediate-release (IR) capsule for on-demand attack treatment, and an extended-release (XR) tablet for once-daily prophylaxis. Pharvaris has no revenue today. It will make money in the US first through specialty-pharmacy sales of a chronic or episodic therapy at a premium rare-disease price.

**Key 2025-2026 events**
- Dec 2025: RAPIDe-3 (on-demand, n=134 randomized per ClinicalTrials.gov) met its primary and all secondary endpoints. Median time to symptom relief was 1.28 h vs >12 h on placebo; median time to end of progression was 17.5 min; median time to complete resolution was 11.95 h. No treatment-related SAEs. Sources: [massbio](https://www.massbio.org/news/member-news/pharvaris-announces-positive-topline-data-from-rapide-3-pivotal-study-confirming-potential-of-deucrictibant-for-on-demand-treatment-of-hae-attacks/), [HCPLive](https://www.hcplive.com/view/pharvaris-deucrictibant-provides-rapid-relief-hae-phase-3-rapide-3-trial).
- 2026-05-08/11: follow-on at $29.68, $115M base. Use of proceeds explicitly includes building a US sales and marketing team.
- 2026-07: FDA accepted the NDA for deucrictibant IR 20 mg capsule; **PDUFA date 2027-04-23** ([BioSpace PR](https://www.biospace.com/press-releases/pharvaris-announces-fda-acceptance-of-new-drug-application-for-deucrictibant-ir-for-on-demand-treatment-of-hereditary-angioedema-attacks), [HCPLive](https://www.hcplive.com/view/fda-accepts-nda-oral-deucrictibant-hae-attacks)). EMA validated the MAA in July 2026. US Expanded Access Program opened (NCT07759141). Peng Lu promoted to President in June 2026 ([Q2 6-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1830487/000119312526345702/phvs-ex99_1.htm)).
- **2026-09-08: CHAPTER-3** (XR prophylaxis, n=85, 2:1 randomization, 24 weeks, 21 countries, the first prophylaxis Phase 3 to include HAE with normal C1-INH): 83% attack-rate reduction vs placebo (p<0.0001); 87% in type 1/2; all secondaries met; no treatment-related SAEs; one AE discontinuation per arm. XR marketing applications are planned to start in 1H 2027 ([finviz PR](https://finviz.com/news/389353/pharvaris-announces-positive-topline-data-from-chapter-3-pivotal-study-of-deucrictibant-xr-for-prophylaxis-of-hae-attacks), [HCPLive](https://www.hcplive.com/view/deucrictibant-xr-meets-primary-endpoint-hae-prophylaxis); [6-K 2026-09-08](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1830487/000119312526384339/6-k_september_8_2026.htm)).

## 3. The "obvious narrative" (the baseline to beat)

A first-pass answer reads: "Pharvaris has the only oral B2 antagonist with positive Phase 3 data in both on-demand and prophylaxis. The pill beats injections on convenience. RAPIDe-3 showed rapid relief (1.28 h) and CHAPTER-3 showed an 83% attack reduction. The NDA is accepted with an April 2027 PDUFA, the cash runway is into 2028, and the stock is a Strong Buy with a mean PT of about $58-59 (about 85% upside)." The Street's 2027 revenue of about $32M and its PTs of $41-$77 are consistent with a probability-weighted multi-billion peak-sales story. The Chiesi buyout of KalVista is widely read as a positive read-across for oral HAE assets ([Fierce Pharma](https://www.fiercepharma.com/pharma/chiesi-lays-out-19b-bolster-rare-disease-offerings-kalvista-and-its-hae-med-ekterly)). This is a crowded Buy. Differentiation must come from quantifying share, price, and timing, not from restating the data.

## 4. Live debates and what public data could settle them

1. **How big is oral on-demand?** The only live comparable is Ekterly (sebetralstat). Net revenue was $1.4M (Q ended 7/31/25), $13.7M (Q3 CY25), about $35M (Q4 CY25, derived from cumulative $49.1M in the 10-KT), and $40.9M (Q1 CY26, XBRL tag `RevenueFromContractWithCustomerExcludingAssessedTax`; the 10-Q fetch summary showed $39.2M, so reconcile) ([KalVista XBRL](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0001348911.json)). Q/Q growth fell from +158% to about +16-18%. Early commercial disclosure: 460 start forms / 253 prescribers in 8 weeks ("nearly 5% of the US HAE population") and 937 start forms / 423 prescribers by late 2025 ([KalVista IR](https://ir.kalvista.com/news-releases/news-release-details/kalvista-pharmaceuticals-provides-update-strong-ekterlyr-launch)). KalVista management's own forecast (in Chiesi's SC 14D9, page 100K+): net revenue $185M in 2026, $446M in 2029, $544M in 2030, peak $705M in 2038, with a DCF of $19.50-$21.70/share vs the $27 deal ([SC 14D9](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001348911/000114036126021078/ny20073033x1_sc14d9.htm)). Settle with: Medicaid State Drug Utilization Data by NDC, Part D spending by drug, Chiesi/KalVista subsequent filings, IQVIA-free proxies such as Google Trends. Also note: Q1 annualizes to about $163M vs the $185M 2026 case, so Ekterly is tracking below management's pre-deal curve.
2. **Share of on-demand: pill vs generic icatibant vs Ekterly.** Pharvaris needs a clinical differentiation argument (speed, 1.28 h median) vs sebetralstat. Cross-trial comparison of sebetralstat time to symptom relief (KONFIDENT) **[UNVERIFIED - cite from the NEJM / label before use]**. Settle with: FDA labels (Drugs@FDA), publication data, ex-US launch metrics once Chiesi reports.
3. **Prophylaxis: late entrant in a crowded field.** Competitors: Orladeyo (oral, Q2-26 net revenue $158.2M; FY26 guide $625-645M; [BioCryst Q2](https://ir.biocryst.com/node/28246)), Takhzyro, Andembry (CSL; reported about US$240M first full year **[UNVERIFIED, search-summary only]**), Dawnzera (Ionis; Q2-26 US sales about $26M, FY26 guide $110-120M **[secondary, verify against the Ionis release]**; [Ionis Q2](https://ir.ionis.com/news-releases/news-release-details/ionis-reports-second-quarter-2026-financial-results-and)), and lonvo-z one-time therapy. Does a daily pill at 83% reduction (n=85, 24 weeks) win share from Orladeyo and from injectables? Settle with: BioCryst new-patient and switch disclosures, Medicaid/Part D volumes, ClinicalTrials.gov comparator enrollment, claims-based switching if accessible.
4. **Intellia timing.** Lonvo-z has a PDUFA six weeks before PHVS. A one-time editing therapy competes for the same prevalent patient pool; payer appetite, price, and safety (liver signals in other in vivo editing programs **[UNVERIFIED for lonvo-z]**) will frame the market. Settle with: FDA decision 2027-03-10, label, Intellia pricing statements.
5. **Pricing and payer.** What WAC will an oral on-demand command, and what gross-to-net (GTN)? Settle with: Ekterly's disclosed GTN in 10-Q/10-K, Medicaid SDUD price per unit, CMS ASP files for injectables, Chiesi pricing.
6. **Financing path.** Does "into 2028" survive a launch? Settle with: KalVista's first-launch SG&A (about $45M in its first quarter; [Nasdaq](https://www.nasdaq.com/articles/kalvista-posts-154-expense-jump-q1)) as an analog.

## 5. Candidate variant-insight hypotheses

**H1 (Short, or avoid Long) - "Oral on-demand is a smaller, slower pie than the Street curve implies, and PHVS is priced for approval plus prophylaxis success."**
- Claim: Ekterly growth has already decelerated sharply (Q1 growth about 16-18% q/q after the stocking quarter and a ramp), and Q1 annualizes to about $163M vs management's $185M 2026 case. A second entrant sharing a market whose first-mover is at about $160M run-rate cannot justify a $1.8B EV on on-demand alone. The prophylaxis option must carry the value, but it enters a market with five or more entrants.
- Financial meaning: PHVS EV about $1.8B vs KalVista's $1.9B take-out for an asset already generating about $160M/yr and with a management peak case of $705M. Sensitivity: each 5 pts of share of a hypothetical $X market shifts value materially; a model with explicit patient and share drivers can show the stock needs roughly the KalVista-case outcome to be fair.
- Datasets: KalVista/Chiesi filings, Medicaid SDUD (NDC level), CMS Part D drug spending, FDA labels, BioCryst quarterly data.
- Evidence so far: for - deceleration math above; against - CHAPTER-3 and RAPIDe-3 are strong, and the pullback from the post-data high has already cut the premium.

**H2 (Short or Long-trade) - "The April 2027 PDUFA is not the binary; Intellia's March 10, 2027 decision is."**
- Claim: A priority-review approval of a one-time curative-intent therapy six weeks earlier re-prices the whole HAE prophylaxis and on-demand pool. Equity investors treat PHVS as a clean approval story; the true path is conditional on lonvo-z label, price, and safety.
- Financial meaning: sets terminal share and prophylaxis peak sales; the 2027 revenue consensus (about $32M) is small, so the stock is driven by peak-sales sentiment.
- Datasets: FDA calendar, Intellia filings, ClinicalTrials.gov HAELO, payer coverage policies.
- Evidence: for - the PDUFA date is public; against - one-time editing therapies have limited initial uptake and cost barriers (typical for gene therapies).

**H3 (Short, event-driven) - "Insider supply overhang."** About 857K shares (about $30.7M) in 16 Form 144 notices were filed from 2026-09-08 to 2026-09-23, including Peng Lu (130K shares, about $4.6M), Johannes Schikan (200K, about $7M), Schoodic Management BV (300K, about $10.5M), and multiple directors (e.g. Bjork 52.5K). 18 Form 4s were filed in September ([EDGAR submissions](https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0001830487.json); each Form 144 is a notice of proposed sale and I did not reconcile to executed Form 4 sales). The Form 144 for Lu on 2026-09-08 implies about $35.25/share (30,000 shares, $1.06M) while a secondary article reports a $40.68 average sale price; reconcile against the Form 4. Claim: selling at this cadence by management and directors is unusual versus the quiet period before the data (only 2 Form 4s and 1 Form 144 in August) and may mark a local top; the stock is about 25% off the post-data high.
- Datasets: Form 4/144 (EDGAR), 10b5-1 plan disclosures in the 20-F, 13F/13G holders.
- Evidence: for - clustered and large relative to float; against - routine post-data sales after option or RSU vesting and plan trades, and the announcement is itself public, which limits any edge.

**H4 (Long, mispricing of the cash-needs shape) - "Approval-year financing is already in the price, but the prophylaxis NDA and EU launch are options not valued."**
- Claim: The market values PHVS at about $1.8B EV with Street 2027 revenue of about $32M, whereas the US XR option (83% reduction, daily pill) could take share from Orladeyo ($158M/quarter) if the label allows switching. The bull case needs the XR to match injectables on efficacy while keeping pill convenience.
- Financial meaning: even a 5% share of an Orladeyo-sized oral prophylaxis pool (about $600M+ annualized) is about $30M+; a model should compare to the 83% vs Orladeyo's historical rates in its label.
- Datasets: BioCryst filings, Part D by drug, ClinicalTrials.gov, FDA labels.
- Evidence: for - efficacy; against - n=85 and 24 weeks is small and longer-term safety unproven.

## 7. Modelability

- **History:** none for PHVS revenue. The analog launch curves are KalVista (5 quarters of Ekterly) and, for prophylaxis, BioCryst Orladeyo (years), Ionis Dawnzera and CSL Andembry (about 1 year). IFRS EUR statements on XBRL since IPO (2021).
- **Drivers a model would use:** US diagnosed and treated HAE population (about 9-10K implied by KalVista's "460 forms is about 5%"; derive and cite properly); on-demand vs prophylaxis mix; share of on-demand by product; attacks per patient per year and attacks treated; doses per attack; WAC per dose and GTN; compliance/persistence for the daily XR; payer step edits; ex-US launches; SG&A build (KalVista's first launch quarter SG&A about $45M); probability of approval; royalty/milestone obligations to BRAIN Biotech (20-F).
- **Data gaps:** WAC and GTN for Ekterly are not disclosed in the 10-Q; no Pharvaris pricing guidance; Intellia pricing; real-world share data (paywalled IQVIA, not usable); the 20-F fetch did not surface specific patient-count statements (read the full Business section **[UNVERIFIED]**).

## 8. Key risks

- **Regulatory:** PDUFA is 2027-04-23; CRL risk, manufacturing/CMC (heavy reliance on third parties per 20-F), label (age range, dosing), REMS.
- **Competition:** Ekterly now owned by Chiesi (deep pockets; tender completed 2026-06-11 at $27/share, about $1.9B; [PharmaFocusAsia](https://www.pharmafocusasia.com/index.php/news/chiesi-group-completes-acquisition)); Intellia one-time therapy 6 weeks earlier; Orladeyo, Takhzyro, Andembry, Dawnzera in prophylaxis; generic icatibant.
- **Financing:** runway "into 2028" per company; another equity raise around approval is likely; the May 2026 raise was the most recent; whether a 2025 raise also occurred was not checked **[UNVERIFIED]** (cash rose from EUR 292M at 12/31/25 to EUR 318M at 6/30/26 after EUR 132M proceeds and EUR 87M burn).
- **Sentiment and flows:** consensus is overwhelmingly Buy; insider sales cluster; M&A optionality (a bid could squeeze any short).
- **Thesis-specific:** secondary-source numbers (Dawnzera, Andembry, short interest) must be verified against primary releases before any pitch use. Aggregator market caps and consensus differ across sites.
